# Trilha do episódio 2: prompt para o Gemini

O vídeo foi montado num grid de 100 BPM (batida 0,6 s, compasso 2,4 s, 15 compassos = 36 s). A música precisa sair
nesse andamento para os cortes caírem no tempo.

```text
Instrumental track for a 36-second high-energy vertical video introducing a crew of pixel-art crab characters from a
Brazilian mangrove. Style: manguebeat from Recife (Chico Science & Nação Zumbi feel): heavy maracatu alfaias and
snare rolls, shakers and agogô, a funky distorted guitar riff and a groovy bass, with small 8-bit chiptune stabs on
top. Tempo exactly 100 BPM, 4/4, strong downbeat on every bar. No vocals, no lyrics.

Structure (bars of 2.4 s):
bars 1-2 (0-4.8 s): starts instantly at full energy on the first downbeat, alfaias hitting every beat, chiptune hook.
bars 3-6 (4.8-14.4 s): main groove with the guitar riff, a small accent on the first beat of every bar.
bars 7-10 (14.4-24 s): same groove, a bit fuller, percussion fills on the last beat of each bar.
bars 11-12 (24-28.8 s): faster feel: accents every half bar (every 2 beats).
bar 13 (28.8-31.2 s): big build with a snare roll into the next bar.
bars 14-15 (31.2-36 s): final drop, one bar of full groove, then a clean final hit on the first beat of bar 15
(33.6 s) and a short ring-out. Must end cleanly.

Mix: punchy, mids present for phone speakers, no heavy sub-bass.
```

Campos separados, se a ferramenta tiver: gênero manguebeat / maracatu / chiptune; BPM 100; duração 36 a 40 s;
sem vocal.
