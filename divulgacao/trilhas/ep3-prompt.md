# Trilha do episódio 3: prompt para o Gemini

O vídeo é um zoom sem fim: a cada 2 compassos a câmera mergulha num mundo novo (prompt, balão, QG, monitor, caixa de
ferramentas, QG no neon), depois recua tudo de volta ao prompt e fecha na tela final. 90 BPM, 13 compassos (~35 s).
Se a música vier noutro andamento, o vídeo é reajustado a ela.

```text
Instrumental track for a 35-second dreamy, curious vertical video where the camera keeps diving into smaller and
smaller worlds inside a pixel-art crab's thought bubble. Style: warm synthwave meets lo-fi, soft analog pads,
arpeggiated synth plucks, a mellow bass, light electronic drums with a gentle swing, tiny 8-bit chiptune sparkles,
a hint of Brazilian percussion (shaker, soft conga). Tempo exactly 90 BPM, 4/4, no vocals, no lyrics.

Structure (bars of 2.67 s):
bars 1-2 (0-5.3 s): starts already in motion, a curious arpeggio, ending with a soft riser.
bars 3-10 (5.3-26.7 s): every 2 bars a new section begins with a clear accent (a "dive" whoosh-like swell and a
small drum fill into it), each section adding one new layer, building slowly.
bars 11 (26.7-29.3 s): reverse-swell, everything pulls back, like rewinding.
bars 12-13 (29.3-34.7 s): bright, happy final section, a clean final hit on the first beat of bar 13 (32 s) and a
short ring-out. Must end cleanly.

Mix: warm, mids present for phone speakers, no heavy sub-bass.
```

Campos separados, se a ferramenta tiver: gênero synthwave / lo-fi / chiptune; BPM 90; duração 35 a 40 s; sem vocal.
